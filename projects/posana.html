Posana Restaurant & Bar

A full visual set for Posana at Biltmore Park, made with Stratton Design Group: the dining room, the oval bar under its cage chandelier, the wine walls and the lounge seating. The bar went from a clay model through several material rounds to the final evening light, and the whole space was cut into a 4K walkthrough. The renders helped settle the design before construction, and the restaurant is open today in Biltmore Park, Asheville.
